Project 02 — 2025 · Data Visualisation · Interaction

Free the Map

An interactive map that shows how much of a city is genuinely public space — and how fast that shrinks once you subtract everything reserved for cars.

Role

Concept, data & front-end

Timeline

6 weeks · solo

Discipline

Data visualisation

Tools

Figma, D3-inspired vanilla JS, QGIS

The brief

Open parking and land-use data for a mid-sized Dutch city, and a question people normally answer with a feeling instead of a number. The challenge was making a dataset feel like an argument.

My approach

I cleaned the data down to two layers: space you can stand in, and space you can only drive on. The scrubber between them is the whole concept — it turns a comparison into something physical you do with your thumb.

The result

Shown at the CMD showcase, where the scrub interaction did more convincing than any label on the page. Built in vanilla JavaScript on an SVG map so it stays fast on a phone.

Deliverables

  • Data cleaning

  • Interaction design
  • Front-end prototype
