Project 06 — 2024 · Service Design · UX Research

Nightline

A service concept for getting students home safely at night, designed around the walk rather than the ride.

Role

Research & service design

Timeline

9 weeks · team of 4

Discipline

Service design

Tools

Miro, Figma, interview kit

The brief

The assignment started as “an app for safe transport”. Eleven interviews later it was clear the anxious part is not the ride — it is the ten minutes before it and the four hundred metres after.

My approach

We reframed the brief and built a service blueprint for those two gaps: a waiting point with light and staff, and a check-in that ends when your door closes, not when the car stops.

The result

The blueprint and a low-fi prototype of the check-in flow. Reframing the question was the actual deliverable, and it is the part I would defend in any interview.

Deliverables

  • 11 interviews
  • Service blueprint
  • Journey map
  • Low-fi prototype
