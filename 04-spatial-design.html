Spatial Design

A design to start.

Role

Concept & execution

Timeline

Year 1 · 5 weeks · solo

Discipline

Experimental design

Tools

Adobe Illustrator

The brief

A project where we got assigned a theme, i got layering & overprinting and a color scheme we had to work with.

My approach

I thought of a theme, energy drinks i got vibrant colors that screamed energy, i then did research on the different ways i could make it more in theme and how i could implement it. i then drew the cans and fruits and implemented it in the design.

The result

The final design incorporated all the research findings and created a cohesive visual identity for the energy drink brand.

Deliverables

  • Designing and iterating
  • Feedback sessions
  • Finding your own style
